# Day 01 — Listening Only: An Uncertain Witness

> **GPT朗读专用英文长对话**。请直接自然朗读下列两人对话，不读标题、注释和说话者标签；语速正常、说话人有轻微区分。**第一次听不展示题目也不翻译。** 本篇为原创模拟，不是官方真题录音。

Officer: We have finally received the witness's statement. She says she saw a man enter the research building at a quarter to eleven.
Researcher: Does she know him personally?
Officer: No. She recognized his dark coat, which matches the description provided by his colleagues.
Researcher: That makes the account worth investigating, but it does not identify the person. Several employees wear similar coats.
Officer: Are you suggesting the witness is lying?
Researcher: Not at all. I am suggesting that we distinguish what she observed from what she inferred. Did she actually see the man open the door?
Officer: Now that you mention it, she said he moved toward the entrance. She then heard a door close.
Researcher: Was there another entrance nearby?
Officer: There is a side entrance behind the laboratory. The records show that someone used a security card there a few minutes later.
Researcher: Interesting. Can we identify whose card it was?
Officer: The system recorded the time, but the card number is missing. I assumed that meant the system had failed.
Researcher: We should not draw that conclusion yet. A missing number could result from a technical error, but it could also indicate that someone removed it.
Officer: So what should we do first?
Researcher: Review the camera footage and check whether the two entrance records are consistent. The decision should be based on evidence rather than a single assumption.
Officer: And if the footage tells us nothing?
Researcher: Then we admit that we do not know. That is more reliable than pretending to be certain.
